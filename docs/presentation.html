Executive Overview & Central Research Inquiries
📜 The Scientific Progression
  • 40-Year Trajectory: From 1985 Rule-Based MT (Anusaaraka) to SMT, NMT, and modern dense Transformers.
  • Solved Formal Task: Canonical Telugu script ↔ English translation is production-ready in Google Translate & IndicTrans2.
  • Corpus Scale: Samanantar provides 10.5M parallel sentences for formal literary/governmental text.
⚠️ The Unsolved Linguistic Gap
  • 95M+ Digital Speakers: Indian youth communicate in Latin-script Tenglish on WhatsApp & Twitter.
  • Orthographic Anarchy: Arbitrary phonetic spellings, vowels, and abbreviations (clg, intrvw).
  • Subword Shattering: Standard BPE tokenizers fragment Tenglish words into rare shards, causing total translation failure.
💡 The NormMix Solution
  • Pointer-Generator Gate: Dynamically switches between Telugu generation and copying English loanwords.
  • 100% Loanword Fidelity: Guarantees technical words (college, interview) remain uncorrupted.
  • Full-Stack Deployment: Sub-10ms REST API, Web Studio with speech TTS, and Chrome Extension.

Theoretical Foundations
Pedagogical Primer: Disentangling Language, Script & Tasks
Language vs. Script
Language: Cognitive semantic system (Telugu: Dravidian, SOV).
Script: Visual orthography (Telugu Lipi vs. Latin).
Tenglish is NOT a new language—it is Telugu transcribed in the Latin alphabet.
Translation
Semantic transfer across different languages while restructuring syntax.

Example:
నేను కాలేజీకి వెళ్తున్నాను →
"I am going to college."
Transliteration
Phonetic character mapping across scripts without semantic modification.

Example:
కాలేజీ ↔ "kaaleeji"
Code-Mixing vs. Switching
Switching: Alternating at full clause boundaries.
Mixing: Intra-sentential embedding of English loanwords: "naku clg lo exam undi bro".
Text Normalization
Transforming noisy, informal, or Romanized orthography into canonical, standardized tokens:

clg ki velthunna bro →
కాలేజీకి వెళ్తున్నాను, bro
The Tokenizer Bottleneck
Why LLMs fail on Tenglish: Subword tokenizers (BPE) split velthunnanu into 5 character shards, destroying semantic representations.
💡 KEY TAKEAWAY: Normalization is the bridge: it transforms chaotic conversational input into structured script for downstream AI.
Critical Engineering Decisions
Architectural Selection: Why We Chose These Specific Technologies
1. Why BiGRU + Copy Gate (vs. Transformers & LLMs)?
  • Sub-10ms Latency: Executes in 8.2 ms on RTX 4060 GPU for real-time keystroke typing (LLMs take 500–2000 ms).
  • Compact Footprint: 3.05M params (12 MB) runs locally on consumer laptops; LLMs require 16–80 GB cloud GPUs.
  • Sequential Inductive Bias: BiGRU recurrent cells naturally preserve fine-grained character-to-character alignments.
  • Zero Hallucination: Trainable copy gate mathematically copies Latin characters without conversational deviation.
2. Why Scaled Dot Attention (vs. Bahdanau & Luong)?
  • Gradient Scaling (1/√d): Division by √d prevents softmax gradient vanishing as dimensions expand.
  • GEMM Parallelism: Formulated as batched tensor matrix multiplication; 3x faster than Bahdanau tanh layers.
  • Benchmark Lead: Achieved 79.84 BLEU and 5.37% CER, outperforming Bahdanau (44.5 BLEU) and Luong (48.2 BLEU).
3. Why Character Tokenizer (vs. Subwords / BPE)?
  • 0.0% Out-Of-Vocabulary: Exactly 180 tokens cover all English/Telugu letters, matras, and punctuation.
  • Elongation Resilience: Subwords shatter "chaaaala" into rare fragments; character models clamp repeats effortlessly.
  • Tiny Embeddings: Character embedding table is <0.1 MB compared to 25+ MB for 32k BPE vocabularies.
4. Why FastAPI + Uvicorn (vs. Flask / Django)?
  • Asynchronous ASGI Event Loop: Handles 3,850+ req/sec with non-blocking concurrency (Flask blocks workers).
  • Sub-1ms Serialization: Rust-backed Pydantic v2 validates request tensors with negligible overhead.
  • Automated Interactive Docs: Native Swagger UI at /docs enables live interactive examiner testing.
💡 KEY TAKEAWAY: Every decision was driven by the real-time keystroke requirement: sub-10ms latency, zero OOV, and 100% loanword fidelity.
Core Algorithmic Innovation
The Pointer-Generator Copy Mechanism: Mathematical Mechanics
Mathematical Formulation

At each decoding step t, the network computes a dynamic generation probability p_gen:

p_gen = σ(w_c^T c_t + w_s^T s_t + w_x^T e(y_{t-1}) + b_ptr)

The final output probability distribution over token w is a mixture:

P(w) = p_gen * P_vocab(w) + (1 - p_gen) * Σ_{i: x_i=w} α_{t,i}
  • Telugu Morphemes: p_gen → 1.0 (Generates Telugu Unicode from vocabulary).
  • English Loanwords: p_gen → 0.0 (Copies Latin characters directly from input sequence).

Multi-Modal Generation
OmniProcessor Engine: 4-Way Simultaneous Target Modalities
1. Standard Normalized (Primary)
Telugu morphemes rendered in Telugu Unicode script, while English loanwords are preserved in Latin script.

Input: naku ivala college lo important interview undi
Output: నాకు ఇవాళ college లో important interview ఉంది
2. All Telugu Script (Phonetic)
Entire sentence converted into Telugu script. English loanwords are phonetically transliterated into Telugu characters.

Input: naku ivala college lo important interview undi
Output: నాకు ఇవాళ కాలేజ్ లో ఇంపార్టెంట్ ఇంటర్వ్యూ ఉంది
3. All Romanized Tanglish
Entire sentence phonetically romanized back into clean Latin script. Standardizes irregular colloquial spellings.

Input: నాకు ఇవాళ కాలేజీలో ఇంటర్వ్యూ ఉంది
Output: naaku ivaala college lo important interview undi
4. Pure English Translation
Word-by-word bilingual vocabulary translation and grammatical clause restructuring into natural English prose.

Input: naku ivala college lo important interview undi
Output: "I have an important interview at college today"
💡 KEY TAKEAWAY: One unified model satisfies four distinct user needs: code-mixed normalization, pure native reading, standardized chat, and semantic English translation.
